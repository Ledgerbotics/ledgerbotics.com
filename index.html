How we work

From first call to reliable monthly delivery

The same structured start for firms and businesses. Firms usually begin with a 7-day pilot; businesses with a free accounting assessment.

    We learn your systems, volumes, deadlines and where you need support.

  2. Scope and match

    We agree a defined scope and match professionals to your work and software.
