About LedgerBotics

Built for CPAs. Delivered globally.

LedgerBotics Global was founded to solve one specific problem: growing CPA firms and businesses need reliable, high-quality accounting talent — but hiring locally is expensive, slow, and inflexible.

You tell us the role — a bookkeeper, tax preparer, fractional CFO, virtual admin. We match you with a handpicked professional, embedded into your workflow, working on your systems, on your timeline. You get the equivalent of a local hire, at a fraction of the cost.

Whether you're a two-partner CPA firm in Ohio, a growing practice in Toronto, a bookkeeping business in Texas, or an accounting firm in Vancouver — LedgerBotics is your invisible, white-labelled back office across the USA and Canada.
